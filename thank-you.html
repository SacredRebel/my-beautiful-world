Before you start

How to read it

It is not a book you read cover to cover. It is a book you point at.

Real pages, straight out of your book. Tap any one to open it full size.

  1. Do not read every language. Pick one or two and stay there a while.
  2. Point first, then say the word. The finger does half the teaching.
  3. Let the colours do the sorting. Each language keeps its own colour on every page, so a child learns which sound belongs to which language long before they can read a letter.
  4. Stop when they stop. Two minutes a day beats twenty minutes once.
